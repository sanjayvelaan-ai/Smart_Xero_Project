# Shop models

## Multifunction copier

`copier.glb` is an original, unbranded model created for PrintFlow OS and dedicated to the public domain under [CC0 1.0 Universal](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). It uses embedded PBR materials and no external textures. The model is 0.84 m wide, 0.815 m high and 0.803 m deep including the projecting keypad. Its front faces +Z and its feet rest at y=0.

| Anchor | Local position in meters | Purpose |
| --- | --- | --- |
| FeedOutlet | 0, 0.470, −0.105 | Center of the paper feed opening |
| OutputStack | 0, 0.434, 0.185 | Center of the bottom sheet of collected A4 output |
| StatusLight | 0.367, 0.679, 0.336 | Dynamic indicator above the control console |

## Characters

Quaternius created the two rigged civilian characters used in PrintFlow. Both artist listings dedicate the models to the public domain under [CC0 1.0 Universal](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).

| Local asset | Artist listing | Original GLB |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `casual-man.glb` | [Man by Quaternius](https://poly.pizza/m/HMnuH5geEG) | [Download](https://static.poly.pizza/3746be88-6799-4817-929b-6bc067c47caa.glb) |
| `casual-woman.glb` | [Woman Casual by Quaternius](https://poly.pizza/m/jpKRgGDxhk) | [Download](https://static.poly.pizza/51d5abdd-bb87-4b8d-9967-21738ffb8437.glb) |

These are locally bundled, stylized low-poly models with embedded materials and skeletal animation. Each faces +Z and includes idle, walk, standing, and sitting clips. Replacement characters should expose equivalent clips and wrist joints. Bind-pose heights are approximately 4.84 and 4.66 model units, respectively.

## Courier uniform

The courier uses the same Quaternius rig with an original yellow shirt treatment, helmet, shoulder straps and insulated delivery bag. The uniform uses Blinkit’s delivery-partner imagery as a visual reference. Its text label identifies the simulated service. The brand name belongs to its respective owner.
